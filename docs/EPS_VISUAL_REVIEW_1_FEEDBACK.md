## Observed issues / proposed direction
- EPS currently looks like amber recolor; secondary violet exists in palette but is not meaningfully visible in screenshots. Design deliberate neutral/amber/violet roles; violet for selected navigation/secondary accents, amber for main intentional action, neutral for ordinary labels. Preserve semantic connection/trust/error colors; do not use brand colors as safety proof.
- Light theme brown primary is contrast-oriented but aesthetically weak. Explore actual gold fill with dark text, darker text accent and violet secondary; test contrast.
- Old green shield on opaque dark square especially conspicuous in light appearance. Reconsider product-specific emblem and launcher art as distinct from fixed EPS publisher lockup; require owner concept approval, no silent replacement or package/signing changes.
- Old header attribution, large vertical spacing, duplicate connect affordances (verify routing), tall Text/PTT cards and large scroll footer; explore a more compact hierarchy without removing accessibility fallback.
- Settings is text-heavy; organize Appearance, Privacy and Data, Application, Advanced; keep security disclosures accessible and truthful. Conversation privacy heading appears partially obscured at bottom of Settings screenshot; investigate rendering/scroll clipping rather than assume cause.
- Preserve exact approved EPS startup art and Field Green palette. No artwork or timing change without evidence/approval.
